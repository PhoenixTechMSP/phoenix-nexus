# Code of Conduct

We want Phoenix-Nexus to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and contribute without friction.

**Expected**
- Be respectful and constructive. Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with questions: many users are IT generalists, not developers.
- Give concrete information when reporting a problem (version, steps, logs without secrets).

**Not accepted**
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam and unrelated promotion.

**Enforcement**
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block contributors who break these rules. To report a problem, email ops@phoenix-tech.be. Reports are handled in confidence.
